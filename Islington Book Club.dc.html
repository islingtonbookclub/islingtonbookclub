The club

Fourteen people who mostly finish the book

We started in a flat off Upper Street in the winter of 2019, when three of us discovered we had all been reading the same novel separately and resenting it privately. It seemed better to resent it together.

Since then we have met every month but two — one for a wedding, one for a burst pipe. We are teachers and nurses and one locksmith; the youngest of us is twenty-three and the oldest will not say.

One book a month, chosen by vote from four nominees any member may put forward. Literary fiction is the centre of gravity, but we have gone out to memoir, nature writing and, once, an entire book about concrete.

Nobody is quizzed. Nobody is shamed for skimming. There is always soup in November and always too much cheese in December.
