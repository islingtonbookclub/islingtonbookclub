To Read To Laugh To Drink

Join us on the last Monday of the month in Islington for drinking, eating, and fun, lively book discussions with a friendly group of adults from all walks of life. Running continuously since 2008, we supplement our usual meetings with a variety of social activities, including an annual literary-themed pub crawl.

Who we are

Running strong since 2008, Islington Book Club is currently led by Rich P, Sam B, Helen M, Susan B, and Sam V. Our members are adults of all ages and backgrounds—whether you're based in North London or coming from further afield, you'll fit right in!

Beyond our monthly book chats, we host year-round socials, including a Christmas dinner, a summer outing, and our famous literary-themed pub crawl.

Membership & joining

We are strictly not-for-profit. A nominal £8 annual subscription covers our website hosting and annual prizes, keeping all regular meetings completely free for active members.

PayPal — send £8 to rsvpickup@hotmail.com with the reference ‘Book Club Subs’.

Bank transfer — no PayPal? Just reach out to Rich for his details.

How saving works {{ publishState }}

Edits below are saved as a draft in this browser and nobody else sees them. Save & preview takes you to the site as it would look; Discard changes throws the draft away and puts back what is published. Publish to GitHub commits the draft to data.json in the repository — that is what every other visitor loads, so it is the step that makes a change real. Pages redeploys in about a minute.

No token to hand? Copy data puts the same JSON on your clipboard to paste into GitHub by hand. Either route ends in the same commit.

Repository and token

The user, repository, branch and file below are published with the site, so once you have set them here and published, every other computer or phone you open Housekeeping on picks them up on its own. The token is the one thing that cannot travel — a token committed to a public repository is cancelled by GitHub within minutes and would let anyone commit meanwhile — so it stays in this browser only. Paste it once here and Housekeeping remembers it on this device, behind the passphrase.

{{ ghSavedNote }}

Make it at Settings → Developer settings → Fine-grained tokens: this repository only, permission Contents: read and write, nothing else. It is kept in this one browser and sent only to api.github.com — keep it in your password manager so you can paste it on a new device. Anyone with access to this device and the passphrase can commit with it — so scope it to the one repo and expire it yearly.
